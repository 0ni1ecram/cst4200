Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • raise PO from awarded line
  • update fabrication %
  • log vessel ETD/ETA
  • record customs clearance

Validation & business rules

  • PO may only reference a line under a signed contract .
  • customs_cleared_at must be set before the shipment is allowed to deliver against an on-site activity.
  • Per-unit embodied carbon is NOT a column in this schema — carbon provenance is carried by the credit ledger (frame 22) and site logs (frame 16), not by material rows.

Roles / states

  • Supply chain coordinator writes; site agent confirms receipt; Owner read-only
  • no shipments booked · customs held · ETA slipped (banner) · partial delivery
