Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • submit daily HSE log
  • record incident / near miss
  • attach toolbox sheet
  • flag stand-down
  • pull drone capture

Validation & business rules

  • LTIFR is per 200,000 man-hours, computed against man_hours from frame 13, not typed.
  • A weather row marking stand-down auto-suggests a non-productive code on the same-log_date daily_progress rows.
  • carbon_kg_co2e here is operational (plant/logistics); embedded carbon is ledger-side (frame 22).

Roles / states

  • HSE officer writes; GC PM co-signs severe incidents; Owner read-only
  • no log today (nag) · incident under investigation · weather feed outage
