Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • re-baseline (snapshots planned dates)
  • insert FS/SS link
  • flag critical path
  • punch activity to a room

Validation & business rules

  • Planned dates are immutable once baselined — a change writes a new baseline row.
  • Float and critical path are recomputed from the dependency graph, never stored by hand.
  • Idle-weather days are matched to site_weather_log.log_date via log_date on daily_progress — there is no stored link column; both dates must fall inside the activity window for an extension-of-time claim.
  • Progress written from frame 13 (daily_progress.percent_complete) drives actual_end.

Roles / states

  • Planner writes; subs read their own activities
  • loop in dependency graph (rejected) · calendar missing · re-baseline in review
