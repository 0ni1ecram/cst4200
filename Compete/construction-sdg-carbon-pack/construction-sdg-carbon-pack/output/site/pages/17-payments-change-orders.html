Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • compile application from EV (frame 13)
  • certify / query line
  • route CO for approval
  • apply contingency draw

Validation & business rules

  • Certified amount never exceeds claimed; each delta needs a reason line.
  • An approved CO over threshold must either consume contingency.allocated_amount (with remaining_amount update) or a funding re-approval.
  • Insurance/bond lapse (frame 11) suspends certification; retention release is a frame 19 gate.

Roles / states

  • QS compiles; PM certifies below delegation; Owner signs above threshold
  • application in draft · certification queried · CO awaiting owner · payless notice
