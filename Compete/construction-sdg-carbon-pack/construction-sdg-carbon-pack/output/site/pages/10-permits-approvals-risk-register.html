Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • register permit & upload decision notice
  • score risk
  • attach mitigation
  • link risk to WBS element

Validation & business rules

  • An expiring permit inside 30 days raises a cross-frame banner.
  • probability_score × impact_score drives the exposure figure; both are ordinal, not percent.
  • Risks linked to site (groundwater, soil bearing) feed the construction method statement.

Roles / states

  • Client representative owns permits; PM owns risks
  • permit refused · expiry imminent · risk closed but linked activity open
