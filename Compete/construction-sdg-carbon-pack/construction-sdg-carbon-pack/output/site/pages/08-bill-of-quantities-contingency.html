Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • add line
  • apply rate set
  • bind line to wbs & element
  • re-roll up to WBS

Validation & business rules

  • productivity_norm drives labour hours; without it the schedule (frame 09) cannot compute duration.
  • Cost rolls up via boq_item.wbs_id and boq_item.element_id , never onto the rate · one WBS node may carry many lines.
  • Contingency transfer into base cost must reference the change_order that consumed it.

Roles / states

  • Quantity surveyor writes; GC reviews awarded rates
  • rate missing · circular unit conversion · unsaved diff
