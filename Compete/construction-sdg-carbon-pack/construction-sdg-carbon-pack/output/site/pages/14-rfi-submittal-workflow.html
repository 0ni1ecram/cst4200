RFI & submittal workflowframe 14

rfi and submittal boards on one clock (submitted_at → due_date → closed_at)

Purpose. Two parallel information channels over the same clock: rfi (question out → answer back) and submittal (document out → review chain back). Ageing is computed from submitted_at / due_date / closed_at; a submittal_review table is not modelled here, so the reviewer chain rides on stakeholder_id plus status notes.

Frame specification (from the wireframe pack)

Primary actions

  • raise RFI against element/activity
  • route to reviewer
  • code answer (A/B/C)
  • close · archive
  • escalate overdue

Validation & business rules

  • Due clock starts at submitted_at ; SLA breach recolours the card and notifies the responsible stakeholder.
  • A submittal cannot be closed while closed_at is null on any open review step.
  • cost_schedule_impact_flag answers require PM countersign before closing.

Roles / states

  • GC raises; design team answers; Owner sees flagged items only
  • board empty · filter by trade · overdue banner · read-only archive
