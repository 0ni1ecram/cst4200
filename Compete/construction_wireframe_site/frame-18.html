Primary actions

  • scan QR to open asset
  • record commissioning reading
  • raise / clear punch
  • book inspection
  • issue COO when green

Validation & business rules

  • Asset cannot close commissioning while a linked punch item is open.
  • municipal_inspection rows key on room and asset; the certificate flag lives only on project.
  • element → asset and system → zone mappings are the asset's own element_id / room_id columns — the dedicated bridge tables named in the design brief are absent from this schema.

Roles / states

  • Commissioning manager writes; facilities lead accepts; authority submits inspections
  • handover checklist 84% · snag storm (bulk open) · inspection failed · COO issued (read-only freeze)
