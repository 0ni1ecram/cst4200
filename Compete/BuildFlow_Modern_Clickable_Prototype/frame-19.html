Primary actions

  • log defect from asset QR
  • assign & track SLA
  • mark resolved with photo
  • approve retention release
  • rebalance PM plan

Validation & business rules

  • retention_release_approved is server-set only: it requires zero open defects and inspection sign-off (frame 18).
  • sla_deadline derives from defect severity class at logging; edits are audited.
  • Every work_order against an asset under DLP is flagged to the responsible subcontractor (contract link).

Roles / states

  • Facilities manager owns gate; subcontractor fixes; Owner releases retention
  • gate blocked · SLA breach banner · dispute raised · all clear (gate armed)
