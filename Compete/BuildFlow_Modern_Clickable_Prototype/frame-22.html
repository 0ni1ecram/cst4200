Primary actions

  • register project & methodology
  • record verified issuance
  • execute P/S/T/R transaction
  • retire with beneficiary note
  • link co-benefit to SDG target
  • export registry statement

Validation & business rules

  • Retirement is irreversible; retirement_beneficiary + registry_reference become part of the permanent record.
  • Sum of transaction quantities may never exceed tco2e_verified of the linked issuance.
  • carbon_credit_sdg_link points at a real project_sdg_target row and its contribution must pass the frame 21 evidence validator.

Roles / states

  • Carbon manager raises; VVB verifies; registry operator settles; Owner approves retirements
  • draft project · verification pending · ledger double-entry mismatch · retired (immutable)
