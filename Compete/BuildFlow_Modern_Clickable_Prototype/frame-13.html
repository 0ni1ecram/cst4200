Data sources (tables → this frame)

daily_progress · 10 cols · 2 FKschedule_activity · 11 cols · 3 FKboq_item · 9 cols · 3 FKcontract · 8 cols · 3 FKbid_package · 5 cols · 1 FKstakeholder · 4 cols · 0 FK

Primary actions

  • submit daily log
  • attach photos
  • recompute EV / SPI / CPI
  • lock history after 7 days

Validation & business rules

  • percent_complete cannot regress unless the prior log is amended with a reason code.
  • A log row without activity_id cannot post to EV; QC hold-point passed (frame 15) is required before % counts on gated works.
  • man_hours should reconcile with headcount × shift length; beyond 2× raises a data-quality flag.

Roles / states

  • Site engineer / superintendent submits; PM approves; subs see own trades
  • offline draft · partially synced · log locked · EV recompute queued
