Data sources (tables → this frame)

bid_package · 5 cols · 1 FKcontract · 8 cols · 3 FKstakeholder · 4 cols · 0 FKproject · 9 cols · 0 FK

Primary actions

  • record bid & prequal score
  • award package → create contract
  • register bond / insurance dates
  • rebid (void award)

Validation & business rules

  • bid_variance_pct beyond ±10% forces a written justification before award.
  • Award requires a prequal-cleared stakeholder_id; retention default from contract.retention_pct, not free-typed.
  • insurance_expiry or bond lapsing suspends payment_application certification (frame 17).

Roles / states

  • Procurement manager writes; commercial/QS verifies; Owner approves awards above threshold
  • packages open · award under appeal · prequal expired
