Trustworthy AI -7 requirements
    - Human Agency & Oversight -> Ai should support not replace, human decision making
    - Technical Robustness & Safety -> Ai should not crash or cause harm
    - Privacy & Data Governance -> Protect, protect personal / individual user data
    - Transparency -> Clear Explanation on how the decision came to be
    - Diversity, Non-Discrimination and Fairness -> avoid Bias
    - Societal and Environmental Welll being -> Benefits Society as a whole
    - Accountability -> Someone has to be responsible when Ai misbehaves

Robustness
 - We have to verify that AI behaves in an tolerable fashion under: abnormal/un-forseen events, Unusual high-impact situations, Adversarial Attacks(mis-reading stickers and signs)
 - Why: Weak models lead to, financial losses, Non-robust models lead to safety risk eg. Self driving vehicle should be able to recognise a vandalised stop sign

Challenges encountered to Achieving Robustness in ML MOdels:
 - Adversarial attacks
 - Distribution shifts
 - Noisy data
 - Generalization
 - Model complexity, if too complex -memorise data, if too simple - misses patterns, require-> Good balance

Adversarial Perturbation:
 - In 2013 small neural networks could be tricked by small invisible changes 
 - Original input; Panda -> classified 57.7% Confidence
   add tiny,                  
 - carefully selected noise; Gibbon -> Classified with 99.3% Confidence

Implications of Adversarial Attacks:
 - Missclassification seems harmless (for Panda to Gibbon)
 - An attacker can search for a tiny change (pertubation) that shift the Models prediction. 
    With the right perturbation the model can be made to output any chosen label
    eg: By tweaking a few pixels on stop sign, classsifier's top prediction flips from(72%) to Speed_45(96%)
        The image should still look unchanged to humans
